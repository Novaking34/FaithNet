== Real-World UX & Wire Gaps Exposed by Session 005 -> Closed in Wave 12

1. **Gap #6 — Periodic `[BEACON RX]` Spam in `faithnet>` REPL -> FIXED:**
   Every 3-second beacon interrupted interactive typing on `Inspiron-5579`.
   Updated `cmd/faithnet-node/main.go` to announce `[PEER DISCOVERED]` **once**
   per newly seen `NodeID` (with optional `--verbose-beacons` flag) and cleanly
   redraw `faithnet> ` after inbound `OpData` messages.
2. **Gap #7 — Duplicate Broadcast Delivery on Same `/24` Subnet -> FIXED:**
   `UDPTransceiver.Send(NodeIDBroadcast)` previously sent both to learned
   `u.peers` (`10.77.77.1`) *and* `u.broadcastAddr` (`10.77.77.255`), causing
   `hello` (`seq=2`) to post twice on The Well. Added `coveredByBroadcast()` in
   `transport/rung3_local/udp.go` to suppress redundant unicast copies whenever
   LAN broadcast already covers that `/24` peer.
3. **Auto-Channel Follower Added to `run_wifi_ap_router.sh`:**
   Polls `wlp3s0` every 5s so switching to a mobile hotspot, another Wi-Fi
   network, or offline blackout (Channel 6 island) automatically retunes `ap0`
   (`FaithNet-HomeRoom`) cleanly—advancing the **"MillenniumOS PC as its own
   built-in Sovereign Micro-ISP"** architecture.
