== Rollout Insight & Wave 9 Solution: 3-Tier Adaptive TLS Trust Ladder

Real browser testing surfaced a crucial rollout psychology insight: **during
community rollouts, visitors must never see scary red self-signed browser
warning screens that make FaithNet look untrustworthy.**

Wave 9 upgraded `interpreter/captive_web.go` and `cmd/faithnet-node/main.go`
with a zero-external-dependency **3-Tier Adaptive TLS Trust Ladder**:

1. **Tier 3 — Public Rollout PEMs (`--tls-cert` & `--tls-key`):** Standard
   CA-signed PEMs (e.g., Let's Encrypt DNS-01 for public/hybrid venues) giving
   every visitor phone a native browser-trusted padlock with zero setup.
2. **Tier 2 — Persistent Sovereign Local Root CA (`--tls-ca-dir`):** Generates
   and persists `ca.crt` + signed leaf `server.crt` covering all local IPs and
   hostnames, downloadable directly via `GET /faithnet-root-ca.crt` for a
   permanent **100% offline green padlock** across home/community devices.
3. **Tier 1 — Ephemeral Emergency Floor:** Zero-config in-memory ECDSA P-256
   fallback ensuring `SSL_ERROR_RX_RECORD_TOO_LONG` never hard-blocks a visitor.
