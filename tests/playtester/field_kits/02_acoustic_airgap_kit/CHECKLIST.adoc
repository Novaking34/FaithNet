= Field Kit 02: Acoustic Air-Gap Speaker-to-Microphone Playtest
:toc: macro

== 1. Objective
Transmit a genuine 512-byte-invariant FaithNet Rung 1 AFSK (1200/2400 Hz) frame across **physical air** from Laptop #1's speakers to Laptop #2's microphone (or loopback cable) with Wi-Fi and Ethernet completely disabled.

== 2. Hardware & Setup
* Laptop #1 speaker volume at ~60% (avoid clipping distortion).
* Laptop #2 microphone positioned 0.3m - 1.0m away in a normal room.
* Both devices in Airplane Mode to prove 100% physical air-gap transmission.

== 3. Execution
1. On **Laptop #2 (Receiver)** start capture first:
+
[source,bash]
----
./tests/playtester/field_kits/02_acoustic_airgap_kit/rx_mic.sh 4
----
2. Immediately on **Laptop #1 (Transmitter)** play the chirp:
+
[source,bash]
----
./tests/playtester/field_kits/02_acoustic_airgap_kit/tx_speaker.sh "HELLO-ACROSS-AIR"
----

== 4. What to Observe & Log
* Compare direct `.wav` decode vs. live room-air capture.
* Note exact impact of leading recording silence / sample alignment (see `SESSION_001_BASELINE.adoc` empirical finding on preamble synchronization!).
* Record room distance, ambient noise, and whether preamble sync upgrade is required for asynchronous mic capture.
