== Straight Answer First: What Is Required vs. Optional?

[IMPORTANT]
====
**You do NOT need walkie-talkies or USB-TTL cables to build, run, or daily-drive
FaithNet!**  
Everything proven in Field Kit 01 (persistent `FaithNet-HomeRoom` Micro-ISP,
`FNP/1` Port `7777` zero-HTTP fetching, The Well `:8077`, dual-stack IPv4+IPv6
WAN firewall, and Zero-`TheTribe` standalone offline mode) **already works on
the two laptops you own today with \$0 extra hardware.**
====

External hardware only enters the picture when you want to unlock **specific
physical upgrades**:

1. **Zero-Blink Wireless Dual-Radio Bridge (Since Your Router Is Upstairs):**
   Right now your laptop uses 1 Wi-Fi radio (`RTL8852CE`) for both upstream
   `TheTribe` and `FaithNet-HomeRoom`. Adding **1 Linux AP-capable USB Wi-Fi
   adapter** gives `FaithNet-HomeRoom` its own dedicated physical radio—so it
   never blinks during upstream roaming or 5 GHz DFS hotspots.
2. **Physical Rung 1 Copper Wire Proof (Field Kit 03 — Optional):**
   Two inexpensive USB-to-TTL serial adapters + jumper wires let you physically
   yank a copper wire mid-transmission and watch RFC 1055 SLIP resync live.
3. **Physical Rung 1 Analog Radio "Across the Street" Proof (Field Kit 03 — Optional):**
   Two license-free **FRS walkie-talkies** + audio cables let you transmit real
   `FNP/1` AFSK/Morse frames across walls or down the street with zero Wi-Fi
   and zero cell towers.

standard **5V USB-C phone battery bank** turns into a portable **FaithNet Pocket
Micro-ISP** you can carry in a backpack or coat pocket:

[WARNING]
====
**Critical Linux Chipset Check Before Buying a Tier 1 USB Wi-Fi Adapter:**  
Many cheap retail USB Wi-Fi dongles use locked-down Realtek chipsets that require
out-of-tree DKMS drivers or do **not** support Linux `nl80211` AP mode!  
Before buying at Micro Center or online, confirm the chipset is **mainline Linux
kernel supported with AP mode**:

* **Best Picks (Plug-and-Play on Ubuntu Linux 7.0, Zero Drivers Needed):**
  * **MediaTek `MT7612U`** (Dual-band AC1200, rock-solid `mt76x2u` in-kernel AP mode — e.g., Alfa AWUS036ACM, BrosTrend AC1L/AC3L Linux edition, Netgear A6210).
  * **MediaTek `MT7921AU`** (Wi-Fi 6E, in-kernel `mt7921u`).
  * **Atheros `AR9271`** (2.4 GHz workhorse, in-kernel `ath9k_htc` — e.g., Panda Wireless PAU05/PAU06/PAU09).
====
