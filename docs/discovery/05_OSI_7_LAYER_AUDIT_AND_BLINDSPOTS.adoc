== Why Run an OSI 7-Layer Blind-Spot Audit Now?

When building a brand-new networking protocol from first principles (`FNP/1`,
Port `7777`, `faithnet://`), it is easy to verify the pieces you *thought* of
(wire framing, Wi-Fi AP, The Well, AI/OmniCode bridge) and accidentally miss a
classic seam hiding in **Layers 2, 3, 4, or 5/6** until you scale to 5+ nodes
or untrusted links.

This audit maps **every single layer of the ISO OSI 7-Layer Model** honestly
against what FaithNet actually executes today—and names the **exact 4 real
blind spots** we should close in future waves.

== Master OSI 7-Layer Audit Matrix (`FNP/1` vs. OSI Reference Model)

[cols="1,2,3,2", options="header"]
|===
| OSI Layer | OSI Responsibility | What FaithNet (`FNP/1`) Covers Today | Honest Status & Blind Spot

| **L1: Physical**
| Raw signals over copper, RF waves, light, or sound.
| Wi-Fi RF (`RTL8852CE` `STA+AP` + dedicated USB auto-detect), Ethernet/`veth`,
  copper UART/TTY (`/dev/pts/*` + `/dev/ttyUSB0`), and acoustic sound
  (`1200/2400 Hz` AFSK + ITU Morse CW).
| **Covered on Binary Hardware.** Native ternary 3-voltage silicon (`-V/0/+V`)
  is not on consumer NICs yet, so `engine/trit.go` bridges binary hardware to
  balanced trits losslessly (`0` allocs/op). *(Fun physics note: 100BASE-TX
  Ethernet actually uses 3-level MLT-3 `(-1,0,+1)` on copper internally!)*

| **L2: Data Link**
| Node-to-node framing, MACs, sync & local wire error detection.
| IEEE 802.11 WPA2-CCMP on Wi-Fi; custom **RFC 1055 SLIP framing**
  (`0xC0`/`0xDB` + double-END resync), Goertzel sliding preamble sync, and
  inline 32-bit **FNV-1a** tamper/corruption seal.
| **Covered — Blind Spot #1 (Optional Rung 1 FEC):** FNV-1a drops 100% of
  bit-flipped frames cleanly, but adding lightweight Hamming/Reed-Solomon
  Forward Error Correction later will let noisy radio/room-air frames self-heal
  1–2 flipped bits without retrying.

| **L3: Network**
| Logical addressing & multi-hop packet routing across networks.
| **Sovereign 64-bit `NodeID` (`0xFA17...`)** independent of IPv4/IPv6;
  128-peer `engine/router.go` (`NextHopID`, `HopCount`); local `10.77.77.0/24`
  DHCP + dual-stack IPv4/IPv6 `FAITHNET_WAN_GUARD` firewall.
| **Partial — Blind Spot #2 (Active Multi-Hop Relay + HopTTL):** `Router`
  tracks multi-hop routes, but `Dispatcher` does not yet automatically forward
  frames where `TargetID != localID` onto `NextHopID` with a `HopTTL` loop
  guard. Needed once we build 3+ node neighborhood chains!

| **L4: Transport**
| Port multiplexing, reliability, ordering, flow control & chunking.
| Canonical **Port `7777`** (`7777/udp` Rung 3, `7777/tcp` Rung 4), monotonic
  `SeqID`, `PingEngine` RTT tracking, passive silent-blackhole cooldown, and
  single-sector `OpFetch`/`OpResource` (`<= 480 B`, `0.26 ms` RTT).
| **Partial — Blind Spot #3 (Multi-Sector Chunking & Retry Over UDP `7777`):**
  Single-sector `<= 480 B` messages/status/bulletins work great today. Sending
  larger payloads (e.g. a 50 KB Nova/Ziv `OpCPISync` snapshot or a 5 MB
  *Tenebris and Lux* patch) purely over native `FNP/1` UDP will need a bounded
  `ChunkIdx/TotalChunks` + selective-ACK reassembly cog!

| **L5: Session**
| Handshakes, peer identity authentication, presence & session resumption.
| `OpBeacon` presence, `--join` handshake, boot-persistent `systemd` auto-rejoin
  (`~3s`), and `nmcli device monitor` roaming continuity.
| **Partial — Blind Spot #4A (Cryptographic `NodeID` Auth on Port `7777`):**
  Legacy HTTPS (`:8077`) has full Tier 2 Root CA TLS, and Wi-Fi has WPA2—but
  raw native `FNP/1` UDP `7777` frames are not yet cryptographically signed
  (`Ed25519`) to prove no rogue peer spoofed Nova's or Ziv's `NodeID`.

| **L6: Presentation**
| Data formatting, encoding, encryption & semantic translation.
| Zero-heap `engine/codec.go` (`50.89 ns`), **Bit <-> Balanced-Trit <-> Bit**
  (`engine/trit.go`, `243` trytes + `256` bytes, `0` allocs), **Genesis 3
  Airlock ($\mathbb{T}_4$)**, and `interpreter/` legacy OS/HTML bridge.
| **Strong — Blind Spot #4B (Native `FNP/1` Payload Encryption):** Pairs with
  L5 above—pure Go stdlib `crypto/ed25519` + `crypto/ecdh` (X25519) +
  `crypto/aes` (AES-256-GCM) can seal native `FNP/1` payloads end-to-end with
  **0 external dependencies!**

| **L7: Application**
| User & model-facing protocols, URIs, portals & services.
| Registered **`faithnet://`** OS URI scheme (`engine/uri.go` + `faithnet-open`),
  zero-HTTP `OpFetch`/`OpResource` (`/well`, `/status`, `/protocol`, `/bulletins`),
  **The Well** (`:8077` captive portal + `/pair.sh` + `/pair.ps1` + binary
  self-replication), interactive REPL, and `OpCPISync` CPI-SI seam.
| **Covered & Live-Verified!** Ready for OmniCode submodule mount, Cornerstone
  C11/C23 codec, and Nova/Ziv CPI-SI application protocols.
|===
