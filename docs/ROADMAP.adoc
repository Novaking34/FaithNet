== First Principles: Breaking the Blindness of Ubiquity

When a technology becomes ubiquitous, humanity stops asking how it works.
People mistake a commercial telecom business model (monthly ISP bills, remote
cloud datacenters, centralized DNS, fragile single-breaker choke points) for
the physical essence of networking.

At its irreducible core, **a network is not a cable owned by a corporation.**
A network is four timeless primitives:

. *Sovereign Identity:* Answering _"Who speaks, and who listens?"_ without
  depending on an ISP-leased IPv4 address.
. *Binary Framing:* Packaging origin, destination, intent, sequence, and
  integrity into a compact, deterministic byte layout.
. *Medium-Agnostic Transmission:* Moving state transitions across whatever
  physical vibration the universe permits (RAM, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, analog radio,
  sound waves, or two copper wires).
. *Echo & Equilibrium:* Verifying presence and temporal latency across the link.
